Isolated practice uses unlimited HP and a quiet boss. Reset or change focus to return to the encounter.

Tile indicators

The cyan square is your position on the current game tick. The character model moves between tiles. Run moves up to two tiles per tick; walk moves one. Use +1 tick while paused to inspect each step.

Equipment switches and movement keep your current weapon cooldown. Select a method, then Clean setup to practise its entry. Watch method demonstrates real tick inputs with guaranteed hits and basic attacks; click the arena to take control. Force next splash lets you practise mage recovery.

Play it like the client

Use Practice settings → Practise a special to try individual mechanics. Show coaching reveals tick counters and timing feedback; Game view keeps the arena clear.

Press the left mouse button on a claw to attack repeatedly. Left-click the floor to move and stop attacking. Switch each item by clicking it in the inventory. Right-click the scene for the action menu.

F4 opens inventory, F5 equipment, F6 prayers, F7 spells. Middle-drag or use arrow keys to rotate the camera. Scroll to zoom. On a phone, tap to act; drag with two fingers to rotate and pinch to zoom.

Show head turns starts a slow demonstration: the player walks across the room, and Olm turns and fires toward each sector. Leaving the demonstration restores 1× speed. Pause freezes the animation; speed controls also slow the turn and projectile movement.

All three hand phases and the final head are playable. Phase three hands can revive; use the phase selector to restart a particular phase.

Build status: actual OSRS room, models and animation frames. The combat and special-attack model is an approximation. Method routes and attack offsets follow the solo guide and have deterministic regression checks on both walls. Damage rolls, random attack selection and some special-effect timings are training approximations; this is not a copy of Jagex’s server.
